The Desk is where you start every visit. It shows who is on the team and what each one is for,
the last ten things that happened, and what wakes each worker. Anything waiting on you sits at the
very top, and only when there is something.

## In the console

Desk, the first screen after you sign in. From top to bottom: anything waiting on you (notes, asks,
failures), the team as cards with a "Chat to" button each, "What happened" beside them, and "What
wakes the team" (clocks and event handlers) underneath. → `#/guide/the-desk`

## The smallest useful thing you can do

Open the Desk, read the top of it, and press "Chat to" on the worker whose job you want to talk
about. The chat starts with that worker's own instructions and tools.

## Ellen's example

Ellen's `newsletter-writer` card says what it is for in one line and that it wakes "every day at
08:00". Under "What happened" she sees it finished an hour ago, and presses "Chat to
newsletter-writer" to ask why this week's draft is so long.

## The glyphs

Filled means a worker did it. Hollow means you did it. A diamond means something is
waiting for you. A cross means a failure.

## The trap

A card's one-line summary is the worker's description, or the first sentence of its instructions
when it has none. Workers can rewrite each other's instructions without touching the description,
so the summary can drift. "Full prompt" shows what the worker actually believes.

## What this will not do

The Desk shows the last ten things, not everything. For every job that ran, and for what the team
has written down, see the rail and the changelog, and Memory.
