The Desk is where you start every visit. It answers three questions: what wants me, what changed,
and what broke. Nothing on it needs decoding: every row says who did it and when.

## In the console

Desk, the first screen after you sign in. Three stacks: Asks, Changes, Trouble. → `#/guide/the-desk`

## The smallest useful thing you can do

Open the Desk each morning before anything else and read
whichever stack is not empty.

## Ellen's example

A filled dot beside "09:14 architect created `newsletter-writer`" tells Ellen
a worker did that, unprompted; a hollow dot beside her own edit later that day tells her she did
that one herself.

## The glyphs

Filled means a worker did it. Hollow means you did it. A diamond means something is
waiting for you. A cross means a failure.

## The trap

An empty Desk is not a sign that something is wrong. "A quiet Desk means the fleet
ran and nobody needed you" (`web/src/components/DeskPage.tsx:260`). Nothing failed, nothing broke,
and nobody has anything to ask you; that is the good outcome, not a symptom of neglect.

## What this will not do

The Desk does not show you everything that ran, only what changed,
what's waiting, and what broke. For every job that ran and the wires that woke it, see the rail and
the changelog and the chart.
